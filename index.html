📋 OFFICIAL RESIDENT COMPLIANCE GUIDE • KEY RULES

Permitted & Prohibited Actions in the Condominium

Direct, practical guidance for condominium living. 6 fundamental rules every adult resident must follow to maintain community order, protect property values, and avoid fines, towing, or legal action.

🚗
MANDATORY REGISTRATION • TOWING RISK

1. Assigned Parking & Towing Enforcement

✔ MANDATORY RESIDENT ACTION: Park only within the painted lines of your assigned deeded space. Every vehicle must display the official barcode decal on the front windshield for automated arm clearance. Guests must obtain a dashboard pass at the gatehouse and park exclusively in marked "GUEST" spaces.
✖ STRICTLY PROHIBITED: Never park in another resident's assigned space (even if vacant). Never park on yellow curb lines, block dumpsters, or park commercial work vans or trailers overnight.
⚖️ DIRECT PENALTY & ENFORCEMENT: Excalibur Towing monitors the lot nightly and tows unauthorized vehicles without warning under Florida Statute § 715.07. Vehicle retrieval starts at $175+ in cash.
🗑️
HEALTH & SAFETY • $100 FINE

2. Household Trash & Chute Guidelines

✔ MANDATORY RESIDENT ACTION: Place all household garbage inside medium-sized plastic trash bags tied securely shut. Open the hallway metal chute door and drop the tied bag directly down the chute.
✖ STRICTLY PROHIBITED: NEVER drop cardboard boxes down the chute. Never force mattresses, mop handles, furniture, or construction debris into chutes, and never leave trash bags on hallway floors.
⚖️ DIRECT PENALTY & ENFORCEMENT: Cardboard lodges in lower floors, creating severe blockages and health hazards. Hallways are camera-monitored: fines are $100 per violation. Flatten cardboard boxes and take them down to ground-level bulk containers.
🔇
QUIET HOURS • 10:30 PM – 8:00 AM

3. Sound Levels & Quiet Hours Policy

✔ MANDATORY RESIDENT ACTION: Keep TV, audio, and conversational volume within reasonable indoor limits. From 10:30 PM until 8:00 AM every night, quiet hours are strictly enforced. Use personal headphones late at night.
✖ STRICTLY PROHIBITED: Never place speakers or subwoofers on balconies or floors that vibrate through concrete slabs. Never host loud late-night gatherings in hallways or common walkways. Never throw a ball against a wall or door (ball bouncing and wall impact noise is strictly prohibited at all times).
⚖️ DIRECT PENALTY & ENFORCEMENT: Senior residents and essential workers reside throughout both buildings. Continued noise disturbances result in immediate dispatch of the Security Gatehouse and City of Miami Police (305-579-6111).
💵
DUE 1ST • LATE AFTER 10TH

4. Monthly HOA Maintenance Assessments

✔ MANDATORY RESIDENT ACTION: Pay your monthly association assessment between the 1st and the 10th of each calendar month. The official and fee-free payment method is online through AppFolio via free bank ACH transfer.
✖ STRICTLY PROHIBITED: Never withhold monthly assessment payments or let payments slide past the 10th of the month. Assessments are statutory legal obligations.
⚖️ DIRECT PENALTY & ENFORCEMENT: Monthly fees fund elevator maintenance, common water, building master insurance, and security. Delinquent accounts incur automatic late fees and interest, and after 30 days are forwarded to legal counsel for collection proceedings.
🛗
48-HOUR ADVANCE NOTICE

5. Heavy Furniture, Deliveries & Elevator Pads

✔ MANDATORY RESIDENT ACTION: When moving in/out or receiving large furniture/appliances (beds, sofas, refrigerators), notify Property Management 48 hours in advance (Monday–Friday, 8:30 AM–4:30 PM) so protective elevator wall padding can be installed.
✖ STRICTLY PROHIBITED: Never move heavy furniture without advance notice. Never schedule moves on weekends or federal holidays, and never prop open elevator doors with brooms or boxes (burns out electric door motors).
⚖️ DIRECT PENALTY & ENFORCEMENT: Elevator repairs cost thousands of dollars. Damaging elevator mirrors, stainless steel finishes, or door tracks will be billed directly to the unit owner.
🐕
LEASH MANDATORY • CLEANUP LAW

6. Pets, Leash Requirements & Waste Pickup

✔ MANDATORY RESIDENT ACTION: Keep dogs on a physical handheld leash at all times when outside your unit (in hallways, elevators, and outdoor grounds). Always carry waste pickup bags and immediately dispose of waste in exterior bins.
✖ STRICTLY PROHIBITED: Never let dogs roam unleashed in common areas or hallways. Never allow pets to urinate on building columns, landscaped planters, or leave unbagged waste on lawns.
⚖️ DIRECT PENALTY & ENFORCEMENT: Immediate statutory fines and neighbor complaints. Miami-Dade County Code and Florida Condominium rules strictly mandate leash control and pet waste sanitation.

Miami River Marina & Boat Slips Information

Essential maritime disclosure for residents, prospective buyers, and boaters. The boat slip docks along the Miami River bulkhead are owned, operated, and maintained by a completely separate private entity, distinct from River Run South Condominium Association, Inc.

⚓
IMPORTANT LEGAL & OPERATIONAL NOTICE Independent Commercial Entity

The Marina is Separately Managed — Not Run by the Condominium Association

While River Run South enjoys prime waterfront vistas along the historic Miami River, the boat slips, wet slips, dock pilings, and maritime dock facilities located along the river seawall are not owned, operated, leased, or governed by River Run South Condominium Association, Inc. or Loyalty Property Management Services, Inc. The marina functions as an entirely independent commercial and private maritime facility under its own dockmaster, corporate ownership, licensing, insurance, and lease regulations.

🚫 Zero HOA Dues Subsidize Marina 📄 Separate Dockage Contracts Required 🚤 No Boat/Trailer Parking in Condo Garage
🏢
CONDOMINIUM JURISDICTION

HOA Dues & Management Scope

Condominium monthly maintenance assessments exclusively fund the 174 residential units, 2 twin towers (2415 & 2475 NW 16th St Rd), residential elevators, 4-level parking structure, 24/7 security guardhouse, building domestic water, and master structural insurance.

No Marina Subsidies: Condominium unit owners do NOT pay for dock dredging, marina pilings, maritime utilities, or marine liability through their HOA fees. Loyalty Property Management does not collect dock rent or oversee boat slips.
⛵
INDEPENDENT MARINA OPERATOR

Slip Rentals & Dockmaster Protocol

Wet slips are leased independently through private dockage contracts. Slip availability, vessel length restrictions, draft clearance along the Miami River channel, electrical shore power hookups (30-amp / 50-amp), and freshwater supply are handled directly by the private marina management.

Direct Dock Inquiries: Condominium purchase or unit lease does NOT include a boat slip. Residents or vessel owners interested in mooring must contact the independent dockmaster/marina operator directly to execute a separate maritime dockage agreement.
🚫
RECORDED BYLAWS & PARKING

No Boat or Trailer Parking on Property

Under River Run South recorded condominium rules (General Rule #17 and Parking Rule #16), boats, boat trailers, jet skis, personal watercraft, and marine equipment are strictly prohibited from being parked anywhere on condominium property, including inside the parking garage or in visitor spots.

Immediate Towing Enforcement: Trailers or watercraft parked on condominium grounds will be towed immediately at vehicle owner expense under F.S. § 715.07. Boaters must keep vessels in wet slips or store trailers off-site at licensed boat storage yards.
🌊
Waterfront Promenade & Private Dock Etiquette River Run South residents enjoy pedestrian access to the paved waterfront seawall promenade. For maritime safety and private property liability, walking onto private floating docks, boarding vessels, or tampering with marina shore power without permission from slip lessees is prohibited.

Parking at River Run South is strictly regulated to protect emergency fire access, prevent overcrowding, and maintain property value:

  • Assigned Parking Spaces: Residents must park solely within their deeded or assigned Limited Common Element (LCE) parking space. Encroaching on neighboring lines is subject to violations.

  • EV Charging: EV charging cords cannot be plugged into building common outlets or extended from unit balconies.

  • No Hanging Over Railings: No laundry, clothing, towels, mops, or rugs may be hung from balcony railings or balcony ledges at any time.
  • White Window Liners: All window draperies, blinds, and window treatments visible from the exterior must feature white or off-white backings/liners.
  • No Exterior Drilling: Drilling, bolting, or anchoring into exterior building stucco, balcony ceilings, or floor slabs (for hammocks, hanging swings, heavy planters, or shade canopies) is strictly prohibited without prior written Board approval to safeguard building waterproofing.

Living in a multi-story waterfront community requires mutual courtesy:

  • Designated Quiet Hours: 10:30 PM to 8:00 AM daily. Loud music, television bass, loud parties, or construction noises are prohibited during these hours.
  • Common Areas: Hallways, stairwells, and elevators are quiet zones. Children must be supervised in common hallways and courtyards.
  • Reporting: Noise disruptions should first be reported to the on-site guardhouse, and chronic nuisances submitted through the AppFolio resident portal.

River Run South is an established, family-oriented community where children and dogs are warmly welcomed! To preserve a clean, healthy, and peaceful home for all families:

  • Welcoming Dogs & Cats: Domestic household pets are warmly permitted (up to 15-20 lbs at full maturity per recorded declaration guidelines and board registration).
  • Leash Safety for Kids & Pets: All dogs must be kept on a physical handheld leash at all times while in elevators, corridors, parking decks, and common walkways.
  • Mandatory Waste Cleanup & Fine Enforcement: Residents with pets MUST immediately pick up, bag, and dispose of pet waste in outdoor waste bins. Leaving pet waste in elevators, stairwells, courtyards, or walkways is strictly prohibited and results in immediate $100 fines per violation (up to $1,000 maximum per F.S. § 718.303).
  • Supportive Community Atmosphere: Quiet hours (10:30 PM – 8:00 AM) ensure peaceful rest for growing families and children.
Mandatory Pet Waste Cleanup & $100 Fine Enforcement Notice

To maintain sanitary grounds for our children and residents, pet waste must be bagged and removed immediately. Failure to clean up after your pet will result in an automatic $100 fine per occurrence levied against the resident, mandatory Board review, and potential loss of pet approval.

Florida Statute § 718.303 sets strict due process standards for condominium fines:

  • Statutory Rates: Fines may be levied up to $100 per day for continuing violations, up to a statutory aggregate maximum of $1,000 per violation.
  • 14-Day Notice & Independent Hearing: No fine may be imposed without at least 14 days' advance written notice and an opportunity for a hearing before an independent Fining Committee composed of residents who are not Board members or officers.
  • No Property Lien: Under Florida law, condominium fines cannot become a lien against the unit. However, persistent violations can result in suspension of common amenity privileges.

Before beginning any interior renovation, flooring replacement, plumbing, or electrical work:

  • ARC Application: An Architectural Review Committee (ARC) application must be submitted to Loyalty Property Management and approved in writing prior to work commencing.
  • Contractor Credentials: Contractors must be licensed in the State of Florida / Miami-Dade County, carry at least $1,000,000 general liability insurance naming the Association as additional insured, and provide proof of Workers' Compensation.
  • City of Miami Permits: Building, plumbing, or electrical permits must be posted on the unit front door as required by City of Miami Building Department.
  • Soundproofing Underlayment: Hard surface flooring (tile, wood, laminate) requires an approved sound attenuation membrane meeting Association STC and IIC sound rating standards.

PART I: GENERAL RULES AND REGULATIONS (ITEMS 1–44)
  1. Single-Family Residential Use: A unit may be used only for single family residential purposes. No unit may be partitioned or subdivided, except in accordance with the provisions of the Declaration of Condominium.
  2. Occupancy Limits: The number of people occupying a condominium unit shall not exceed the amount permitted by applicable zoning regulations promulgated from time to time by the appropriate governmental authority.
  3. Reserved / Unassigned.
  4. Exterior Modifications & Flags: The exterior of the building and all areas appurtenant to the condominium shall not be painted, decorated or modified by any unit owner in any manner without the prior consent of the Association, which consent may be withheld on purely aesthetic grounds within the sole discretion of the Association. However, pursuant to section 718.113(4), Florida Statutes, Unit Owners are permitted to respectfully display a United States flag and on Armed Forces Day, Memorial Day, Flag Day, Independence Day and Veterans Day may display in a respectful way portable, removable, official flags, not larger than 4 ½ feet by 6 feet, that represent the United States Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps or Coast Guard, regardless of any rules or requirements dealing with flags. No awnings, window guards, light reflective materials, hurricane or storm shutters, ventilators, fans or air conditioning devices shall be used in or about the building except as shall have been approved by the Association.
  5. Window Coverings & Liners: Installation of drapes or curtains visible from the exterior of the unit shall have white or off-white, black out type liners used, which liners must be approved by the Association.
  6. Supervision of Children: Children of guests shall at all times be supervised by their parents or the Unit Owner they are visiting. Children shall not be permitted to play or loiter in stairways, sidewalks or other Common Elements.
  7. Common Element Walkways: The sidewalks, catwalks, entrances, passages, fire exits, patios, stairways, and like portions of the Common Elements or Limited Common Elements shall not be obstructed and shall not be used for any purpose other than ingress and egress; nor shall carts, carriages, bicycles, chairs, tables or any other similar objects be stored therein.
  8. Personal Property Storage: The personal property of Unit Owners must be stored in their respective units.
  9. Balconies & Laundry Drying: No garbage cans, supplies, or other articles shall be placed in or on the patios, balconies or staircase landings, nor shall any linen, blankets, clothing, curtains, rugs, mops, or laundry of any kind or other articles, be shaken or hung from any of the windows, doors, patios or balconies. No visible clothes lines or other outside facility for drying or airing clothes shall be erected.
  10. Shaking Rugs & Dropping Objects: No cloth, clothing, rugs or mops shall be hung open or shaken from windows or doors. No Unit Owner shall permit anything to fall from a window or of a Unit, or sweep or throw from the Unit any dirt or other substance into any of the sidewalks, patios or Common Elements.
  11. Trash Disposal: All garbage must be deposited in bags with all other refuse in areas designated for such purpose.
  12. Skateboarding & Bicycles: No skateboarding or bicycle riding shall be permitted in the Common Elements or on the Condominium Property.
  13. Association Employees: Employees of the Association may not be sent by Unit Owners for personal errands. The Board shall be solely responsible for supervising Association employees.
  14. Inoperable Vehicles: No motor vehicle which cannot operate on its own power shall remain on the Condominium Property for more than forty-eight (48) hours.
  15. Vehicle Repairs: No vehicle shall be repaired on the Condominium Property.
  16. Prohibited Parking: No trucks, trailers, mobile homes, vans, campers, buses, motorcycles or similar vehicles shall be parked on the condominium Property.
  17. Boats & Watercraft: No boats, rafts, canoes or other similar craft shall be allowed on the Condominium Property.
  18. Balcony Cleanliness & Furnishings: All balconies and decks shall be kept in an orderly, clean and sanitary fashion at all times. Consistent with the foregoing, the placement of any chairs, benches and tables on same shall be of such a number, nature and type as are customarily used for leisure purposes and in all cases subject to the Board's prior approval.
  19. Disruptive Conduct: No Unit Owner shall make or permit any disruptive noises or noxious fumes in the buildings, or permit any conduct of any persons that will interfere with the rights, comforts or conveniences of other residents.
  20. Musical Instruments & TV Volume: No Unit Owner shall play or permit to be played any musical instruments, or operate or permit to be operated a phonograph, television, radio or sound amplifier in his Unit, porch balcony or patio in such a manner as to disturb or annoy other residents.
  21. Vocal Instruction: No Unit Owner shall conduct or permit to be conducted vocal or instrumental instruction at any time which disturbs other residents.
  22. Quiet Hours (10:30 PM to 8:00 AM): Radios, televisions and other instruments which may create noise should be turned down to a minimum volume between the hours of 10:30 p.m. and 8:00 a.m. All other unnecessary noises, such as bidding good night to departing guests and slamming car doors between these hours should be avoided.
  23. Interference with Reception: No radio or television installation shall be permitted in a Unit, balcony or patio which interferes with the television or radio reception of another unit.
  24. Antennas & Aerials: No antenna or aerial maybe erected or installed by a Unit Owner on the roof or exterior walls of the buildings.
  25. Signs & Notices: No sign, advertisement, notice or other lettering, except signs used by Developer, shall be exhibited, displayed, inscribed, printed or affixed in, on or upon any part of a Unit which may be seen from the Common Elements pursuant to Section 718.103 (70 F.S.).
  26. Cooking Restrictions: Cooking shall be allowed only in the kitchen of each Unit and within those Common Elements of the Condominium Property which are designated by the Board for such use.
  27. Banners & Pictures: No signs, pictures, banners, posters or other objects of any nature shall be displayed from, affixed to, or painted upon a Unit or the Common Elements.
  28. Plant Diseases & Insects: No Unit Owner shall permit any condition to exist which shall induce, breed or harbor plant diseases or noxious insects.
  29. Flammable Substances: No flammable, combustible, or explosive fluids, chemicals or substances shall be kept in any Unit, balcony or patio, except as maybe required for normal household or permitted business use.
  30. Hurricane Season Absence: A Unit Owner who plans to be absent during the hurricane season must prepare his Unit prior to his departure by removing objects from patios and designating a responsible local contact.
  31. Consumption Areas: Food and beverages may not be consumed outside of a Unit and its appurtenant patio(s) or balcony except in designated areas.
  32. Enclosing Balconies: A Unit Owner shall not cause anything to be affixed or attached to, hung, displayed or placed on the roofs exterior walls, patios, or windows, nor shall any Unit Owner screen or otherwise enclose his balcony or patio.
  33. Window Ledge Objects: Plants, pots, receptacles and other movable objects must not be kept, placed or maintained on ledges of windows. No object shall be hung from window sills.
  34. Throwing Objects from Windows: Unit owners shall not throw cigars, cigarettes or any other objects from windows or doors.
  35. Pets & Animals: No PETS shall be maintained or harbored within a condominium unit, except as permitted per recorded community guidelines and F.S. 718.303.
  36. Drilling Floors & Walls: No drilling of floors, patios, exterior walls or ceilings is allowed for attachment or hanging of any material, including planters and hammocks, without prior written approval.
  37. Door Mats & Holiday Decor: No door mats may be placed outside of a Unit and no ornaments or decorations may be hung on the exterior walls, gates or fences, except for U.S. flags permitted under F.S. 718.113(4).
  38. Commercial Business Prohibition: No commercial or business purpose shall be conducted or solicited in any Unit.
  39. Window A/C Units: No Unit Owner may install or permit to be installed any window air conditioning unit in his Unit or in Common Elements.
  40. Moving Scheduling: No Unit Owner may schedule the moving of furniture or furnishing into or out of the Condominium unless scheduled with management to assure parking and access.
  41. Window Films & Reflective Glass: No Unit Owner shall attach any film or sun-reflective device or matter to the glass windows and glass doors without Board approval.
  42. Balcony Planter Heights: No plantings in a balcony or patio which can be observed over the horizontal or vertical planes of patio walls are permitted.
  43. Enforcement Costs & Attorney Fees: Those Unit Owners who violate these rules shall be responsible for all costs incurred by the Association, including court costs and reasonable attorney fees.
  44. Fining Procedures & Due Process: The Association shall provide reasonable notice and a hearing before levying a fine up to $100 per day / $1,000 aggregate before a committee of other unit owners.
PART II: PARKING RULES AND REGULATIONS (ITEMS 1–25)
  1. Designated Spaces Only: The streets within the Association shall not be used for vehicular parking and all vehicles shall be parked in a designated parking space, out of the right of way.
  2. Deeded Space Parking: Owners and residents shall park their motor vehicles in the parking space assigned to their unit without encroaching in the right of way or adjacent parking spaces.
  3. Motorcycles & Scooters: Motorcycles and scooters are not allowed within the condominium's parking area.
  4. Parking Permits / Decals: With the exception of emergency vehicles, every vehicle shall display the Association's parking permit in a conspicuous place.
  5. Visitor Spaces: Guests of owners/residents shall park in those parking spaces designated as "Visitor."
  6. Guest Stay Limits: No guest shall park in a "Visitor" parking space for more than a total of 4 overnight days each 10 calendar days without requesting advance permission.
  7. Visitor Sign Display: Any vehicle driven by a guest must prominently display a "Visitor Sign".
  8. Towing of Unregistered Guest Cars: Any guest vehicle parked in a space that is not designated as "Visitor" parking shall be subject, without notice, to immediate towing.
  9. Fire Lanes & Obstructions: Parking in a fire lane, in front of ingress and egress points, or emergency access lanes is expressly prohibited and subject to emergency towing without notice.
  10. Handicapped Parking: No vehicle shall park in a Handicapped space unless displaying a valid Disabled Parking Permit or License Plate.
  11. No Assignment of Spaces: Owners or registered residents may not assign, rent or otherwise allow for the use or transfer of their parking spaces to other Owners or Residents.
  12. Clear Ingress & Egress: Vehicles shall be parked such that there is no interference with other motor vehicles or pedestrian pathways.
  13. Size & Weight Limits: No vehicle exceeding 19 ft in length, 6 ft 8 in in height, or weighing in excess of 6,000 lbs shall be allowed to park in the building.
  14. Mandatory Registration: Vehicles must be registered with the association and belong to the occupant in the unit.
  15. Space Boundaries: The vehicle must fit within the boundaries set by its parking space lines drawn on the floor.
  16. Commercial Vehicles: No commercial vehicles (used for business purposes) shall be allowed to park.
  17. Compliance Grace Period: Vehicles presently in violation shall be given sixty (60) days to correct said violations.
  18. Abandoned or Inoperable Vehicles: No abandoned or inoperable vehicles (lacking current registration/tags, or not driven for 72+ hours) shall be stored or parked in the Association.
  19. No Vehicle Maintenance: No maintenance, repair, rebuilding, dismantling, repainting, or servicing of any kind on any vehicle may be performed anywhere in the Association.
  20. No EV Charging from Units: No electrical vehicle shall be charged from any electrical source originating from the Unit of an owner or resident.
  21. Cumulative Board Enforcement: The board may enforce these rules in accordance with the Declaration, Bylaws, and Florida Law, including fines and towing.
  22. Towing Risk & Notice: Any vehicle parked in violation may be towed at the sole risk and expense of the owner after 24 hours of written notice (or immediately for fire lanes/handicap/unauthorized visitor spots).
  23. Fining Limits: The Association may impose fines not exceeding $100.00 for each day the violation continues, up to $1,000.00 in the aggregate.
  24. No Circumvention / Rotation: Rotation of vehicles owned by the same owner/resident for the purpose of circumventing these rules is prohibited.
  25. Non-Waiver: Failure of the Association or its board to enforce any of these Rules and Regulations shall in no event be deemeda waiver of the rights to do so thereafter.
